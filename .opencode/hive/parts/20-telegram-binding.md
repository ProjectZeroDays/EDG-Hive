# 20 — Telegram binding

**Goal:** Reply and control from Telegram.

**Steps**
1. Per-user bot link, one-click unlink revoking the binding.
2. Messages mirror both directions into the Users tab.
3. `!ai` prefix or a toggle routes text to the agent; replies return to Telegram.

**Done when**
- Round-trip works both ways; unlinking stops delivery.
